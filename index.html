What this network includes

This is a curated, source-backed sample, not a complete alumni directory or a ranking of economics. Only explicitly documented doctoral advisers, co-advisers, and dissertation committee relationships are drawn. Collaboration, intellectual influence, undergraduate supervision, and sharing a university do not by themselves create an edge. Missing links reflect research coverage, not the absence of students. Career summaries are tied to their cited sources and check dates; historical appointments are identified as such.

Life stories are short sourced accounts in each laureate’s profile. Personal stories are kept separate from academic relationships.

ABOUT THIS ATLAS

Big ideas deserve
an open door.

This independent educational archive brings together every recipient of the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel from 1969–2025.

Names, award years, and award-time affiliations come from NobelPrize.org. Research fields are editorial browsing tags, and contributions are explained in plain language. Each profile connects you to original sources, documented academic relationships, and a short life story.
